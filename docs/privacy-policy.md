# Privacy Policy

**PlacePulse** is an unofficial, open-source companion app for BIT Mesra's
Training & Placement (T&P) portal. It is developed and maintained by Sachin
and made available to students free of cost.

This page explains what information PlacePulse collects, how it is used,
and what it does not do with your data.

## Information We Collect

PlacePulse does **not** require you to sign in, and does **not** collect
any personally identifiable information such as your name, roll number,
email address, or T&P portal credentials.

The app only reads publicly available job listing data (company name,
deadline, posting date, application links) from the BIT Mesra T&P portal
and displays it inside the app.

## Push Notifications

PlacePulse uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send you a notification
when a new job is posted to the T&P portal. Your device subscribes to a
single shared notification topic (`new-jobs`). This subscription is
anonymous — we do not know who owns which device, and no personal data is
attached to it. You can disable notifications at any time from your
device's app settings.

## Third-Party Services

PlacePulse relies on the following third-party services to function:

- **Firebase Cloud Firestore** — stores the job listing data shown in the
  app.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — delivers push notifications.

These services are provided by Google. Their use of data is governed by
[Google&#39;s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not
share any additional information with them beyond what is technically
required for the app to function (e.g. your device's notification token,
which Google's infrastructure uses to route the push).

## Log Data

Like most apps, basic technical crash/error logs may be generated on your
device for debugging purposes. This data stays local to your device and
is not transmitted to or collected by us.

## Children's Privacy

PlacePulse is intended for use by BIT Mesra students and does not
knowingly collect information from anyone. The app has no age gating
since no personal information is collected in the first place.

## Data Security

Since PlacePulse does not collect or store personal information on any
server we control, there is no personal user data at risk of exposure
from our side. Job listing data is publicly available information
already published by the T&P portal.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Changes will be reflected
on this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted
constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

This policy is effective as of **August 19, 2026**.
